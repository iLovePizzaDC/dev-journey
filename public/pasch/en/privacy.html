Pasch

Privacy

Pasch is a dice game that runs on your device. There is no Pasch account and no Pasch server that holds your game data.

The provider is Nico Betz, Ofengasse 2, 71336 Waiblingen. Contact: n.betz1102@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Your display name, appearance, sound and haptics preferences, rule preference, lifetime game statistics, and an unfinished solo or same-device game are stored on this device and synced through your Apple iCloud account (Key-Value storage) when you’re signed into iCloud — so they stay the same across your devices. Nearby games are not saved. You can reset statistics in Settings. Delete the app to remove the local copy; data may remain in iCloud until you clear it from your other devices or iCloud settings.

Nearby multiplayer

When you host or join a nearby game, Pasch uses Apple Multipeer Connectivity over Bluetooth and local Wi‑Fi. Devices exchange your chosen display name and live game state (dice, scores, turns) only with players you connect to nearby. Nothing is sent to a server we run. Local Network permission is required for this feature. You can leave a game at any time; the connection then ends on this device.

No ads, no tracking

Pasch does not sell data and does not show ads. There are no analytics SDKs from us.

Your rights

You can clear your name in Settings or remove the app. For access or deletion requests: n.betz1102@gmail.com. Nearby play also follows Apple’s privacy rules for Multipeer Connectivity and Local Network access.
