Höher

Privacy

Höher is a dice game that runs on your device. There is no Höher account and no Höher server that holds your game data.

The provider is Nico Betz, Ofengasse 2, 71336 Waiblingen. Contact: n.betz1102@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Your name, the name across the table, appearance, sound and haptics preferences, number of lives, difficulty, and lifetime statistics are stored on this device and synced through your Apple iCloud account (Key-Value storage) when you’re signed into iCloud — so they stay the same across your devices. You can reset statistics in Settings. An identifier for nearby games stays on this device only, so two devices on the same Apple ID can still play each other. Delete the app to remove the local copy; data may remain in iCloud until you clear it from your other devices or iCloud settings.

Nearby play

When you host or join a nearby game, Höher uses Apple Multipeer Connectivity over Bluetooth and local Wi‑Fi. Devices exchange your chosen name and the live game only with the people you connect to. Dice someone else is not allowed to see stay covered on their device. Nothing is sent to a server we run. Local Network permission is required for this feature. You can leave a game at any time; the connection then ends on this device.

No ads, no tracking

Höher does not sell data and does not show ads. There are no analytics SDKs from us.

Your rights

You can change your name on the home screen or remove the app. Reset statistics in Settings. For access or deletion requests: n.betz1102@gmail.com. Nearby play also follows Apple’s privacy rules for Multipeer Connectivity and Local Network access.
