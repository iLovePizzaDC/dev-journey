Mealpin

Privacy

Mealpin stores your recipes and weekly plan on this device. There is no Mealpin account and no Mealpin server that holds your data.

The provider is Nico Betz, Ofengasse 2, 71336 Waiblingen. Contact: n.betz1102@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Recipes (title, notes, ingredients, steps, optional source URL), planned meals, settings (for example your chosen Reminders list), and optional NFC bindings. If you delete the app, local data is gone — unless it is already in your iCloud.

iCloud

When iCloud is available, Mealpin can sync recipes and plans across your devices with Apple CloudKit. Sync data lives in your Apple iCloud account, not on a server we run. You can see the sync status under More.

Sharing your plan (optional)

If you share your plan, Mealpin puts a copy of the current weeks and the recipes they use into your iCloud and gives the people you invite read access through Apple CloudKit sharing. They can see that plan and those recipes and copy recipes into their own library. Invited people are visible to you in the app. You can stop sharing at any time under Plan → Sharing; access then ends for everyone.

Apple Reminders

When you export a shopping list, Mealpin writes items into the Reminders list you choose. That needs your permission. You manage those items in Apple Reminders (on device and/or in your iCloud). We cannot see your lists.

NFC (optional)

NFC tags only store a short ID / deep link (mealpin://…), never recipe content. The current plan is resolved in the app when you tap a tag.

Web import

URL import briefly loads a page to read recipe data. What you keep is saved as a recipe on this device. The site operator remains responsible for their page.

In-app purchases

Purchases go through Apple. Apple processes payment, knows your Apple Account, and restores purchases. We do not see your payment details.

No ads, no tracking

Mealpin does not sell data and does not show ads. There are no analytics SDKs from us.

Your rights

You can delete data in the app or remove the app. For access or deletion requests: n.betz1102@gmail.com. For iCloud, Reminders, and purchases, Apple’s rules also apply.
